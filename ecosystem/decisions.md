# Decisions

Decisions about this ecosystem (not about the engine — those are ADRs in `docs/adr/`):
creating, splitting, freezing or detaching a module, rejecting a pending item, choosing between
real alternatives. Newest first. History of everything else is in git; automatic actions are
the commits prefixed `auto:`.

Entry format:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — short title
- **Where:** module(s) or core
- **Options:** what was on the table
- **Chosen:** what we do, and why
```
